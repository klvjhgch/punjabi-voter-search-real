Real PDF Processing

PDFs with embedded text are extracted directly. Scanned PDFs use Punjabi + English OCR. Records are stored in SQLite with source page numbers and photo coordinates. Serial numbers come from the boxed voter-card number; marker strings such as 16/20/xxx and ws-xxxx are never used as Serial or Part.
